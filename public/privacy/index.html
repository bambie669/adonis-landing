Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026
This document is a plain-language summary of how Adonis handles your data. It is provided in good faith and kept current, but it is not legal advice — for a binding agreement, have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction.

Adonis ("Adonis", "we", "us") is an intelligent ads platform: it analyzes video ads, generates content, publishes to social platforms, audits pages, and surfaces leads. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — your email address and organization, used to create and secure your account.
  • Connected platform credentials — when you connect Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or Google, we store the access tokens those platforms issue. Tokens are encrypted at rest and used only to perform the actions you request (reading your accounts, publishing your content).
  • Content you create or upload — the ad URLs you analyze, media you upload, captions, scripts and posts you generate and schedule.
  • Usage and diagnostic data — basic logs and error reports used to keep the service running and secure.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the service — analyze ads, generate content, publish and schedule posts, run page audits, and surface leads.
  • To secure your account and prevent abuse.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the platform.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-party platforms

When you use a feature that touches another service, we exchange data with that service on your behalf and under your authorization:

  • Meta (Instagram & Facebook) — to read your linked accounts and publish content you create.
  • TikTok — to read your account and publish content.
  • Google (YouTube, Gemini) — to ingest public video and generate analysis.

Your use of those platforms through Adonis is also governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Data retention & deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can disconnect any platform at any time from the Connections page, which revokes and removes the stored tokens. To request deletion of your account and associated data, see our data deletion instructions or contact us at the address below.

5. Security

Platform credentials are encrypted at rest, access is authenticated with signed tokens, and we follow reasonable technical measures to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep yours safe.

6. Your choices

  • Disconnect any connected platform at any time.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your data.
  • Contact us with any privacy question.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.
